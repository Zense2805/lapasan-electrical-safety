# Proposed survey and nonintrusive fieldwork blueprint

This is an **instrument drafting aid**, not a validated research instrument or finalized participant consent document. Review with the project leader, barangay focal person, privacy officer / designated data steward and relevant safety advisers before deployment.

## Consent information to provide BEFORE answering

Include a plain-language, locally appropriate notice covering:

1. Project purpose and institution/partners actually involved (do not imply BFP endorsement until confirmed).
2. What information is requested, collection methods and approximate survey duration.
3. Participation is voluntary; respondents may skip a question (where appropriate), decline or stop with no negative consequence.
4. No technical inspection is being performed and respondents must not touch live electrical equipment to answer.
5. Who can access identifiable/private records, how long they will be kept, where stored and how a participant can raise concerns or seek permitted withdrawal.
6. How responses will be aggregated and used for planning, and that public maps do **not** identify homes or establish safety compliance.
7. A safe, separate channel for urgent hazards and the limits of this website (not an emergency-report service).
8. Project contact channels, only after officially confirmed.

A check box may record consent **only if the process is approved**. Do not substitute this blueprint for an institutional ethics/privacy review if required.

## Proposed questionnaire domains and examples

**Area and dwelling context**
- General *approved survey zone code*; unknown/prefer-not-to-answer option. Do not collect house number, unit number or exact coordinate through the public form by default.
- General dwelling/occupancy profile as agreed in the instrument pilot.

**Electrical use and existing warning signs**
- Frequency of extension-cord / adapter use (often/sometimes/rarely/unknown or prefer not).
- Previously observed fraying, overheating, repeated tripping, smoke, sparks, odor (each can use yes/no/unknown or prefer not, ideally separately to avoid contradictory multi-select choices).
- Previously reported suspected electrical incidents (self-report only, no causation inference).
- Knowledge of a qualified repair/referral route and barriers to seeking professional assistance.

**Preparedness and contextual access**
- Household awareness of evacuation routes, emergency numbers and safe practices.
- General evacuation or emergency-access constraints without identifying vulnerable individuals.
- Option for unknown or prefer not to answer.

**Feedback and follow-up**
- Open suggestions that do not request names or addresses.
- If individual follow-up is needed, collect identifiers **separately** with an approved privacy and referral process. Do not put them in the map export.

## Nonintrusive fieldwork checklist

- Observe only common areas or places respondents explicitly permit.
- No opening of electrical panels, energized testing, plugging/unplugging equipment for survey purposes, removal of safety covers, or technical diagnosis.
- Record observations as visible or self-reported **screening indicators**, not as confirmed defects or code violations.
- Establish an urgent-hazard escalation route BEFORE collecting reports, as specified in the proposal.
- Supervisors and students must know when to stop the observation and refer to qualified personnel.
- Offer assisted or paper collection for households without internet, low digital literacy or accessibility needs.
- Avoid photographing dwelling identifiers or occupants unless specifically justified and permissioned; never publish identifiable images casually.
- Keep coded field logs private, in project-controlled storage; do not add raw logs to this GitHub repository.

## Survey piloting and quality checks

Verify Cebuano/Filipino/English wording as appropriate, comprehension, response options, response burden, zone-code accuracy, handling of unknowns and support for assisted surveys. Prevent duplicate or contradictory survey records using an agreed **privacy-safe** verification process rather than compulsory collection of email addresses.
