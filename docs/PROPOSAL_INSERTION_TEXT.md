# Optional paragraph for the original proposal

Suggested insertion under **III. Project Components and Activity Design**, after the existing four components. This is draft wording to be approved by the project leader. It is **not** an authorized modification to the official USTP document.

> **Digital Assessment and Information Support.** A mobile-responsive website hosted through GitHub Pages may be used as a public information portal and entry point to an approved, voluntary household questionnaire. The survey responses shall be collected and stored separately in a restricted-access project account, and not in the public website or repository. The website may present project orientation materials, electrical safety information, approved zone-level vulnerability maps, an indicator interpretation guide, and the community action-plan summary. Public release of findings shall follow data quality review, aggregation, privacy screening, and stakeholder validation. Digital collection will complement, rather than replace, supervised nonintrusive field observations, assisted or paper survey options, professional hazard-referral arrangements, and action-planning and turnover activities.

Optional short addition under **IV. Project Implementation Plan and Management**:

> The technical team shall prepare and pilot the approved online survey during Weeks 1-2, support digital data collection and secure file management during Weeks 3-6, prepare privacy-reviewed GIS outputs during Weeks 7-9, and turn over the public information site and maintenance instructions with the approved action plan by Week 12. Website publication and partner responsibilities are subject to approval.

Do **not** change budgets, project dates, named proponents or partner commitments without confirmation. The site needs a real maintenance owner, even if hosting is free.
