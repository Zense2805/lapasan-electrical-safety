## Crucial boundaries from the proposal

- The map is a **screening tool** and should not imply that a particular dwelling is safe/unsafe or determine fire causation, code compliance or electrical defects.
- No panel opening, energized testing, intrusive inspection or unsupervised technical assessment by students.
- Scope and sample, public area boundaries, permissions, partner duties, agency representation, budget and implementation dates are **not final**.
- Barangay Lapasan is the project location. San Roque and San Isidro are only possible field routes if agreed; do not preset them as official survey areas.
- Bureau of Fire Protection participation remains **subject to confirmation**.
- Avoid actual household markers and low-count/public individual data.

## Work packages mapped to the 12-week proposal

- **Weeks 1-2:** website review; finalize the data privacy plan and referral protocol; validate and pilot questions; create project-controlled Google Form and restricted Sheet.
- **Weeks 3-6:** activate authorized Form, distribute QR code, offer assisted/paper collection, supervise nonintrusive observation and data quality review.
- **Weeks 7-8:** clean/analyze private responses, calculate reviewed indicators with documented treatment of missing data, create QGIS aggregates and sensitivity check.
- **Week 9:** present draft maps to stakeholders and revise as needed.
- **Weeks 10-11:** co-design action plan, orientation on interpreting the map, referrals and monitoring.
- **Week 12:** approve public outputs, publish safe summaries, turn over files, ownership, training notes and evaluation records.

## How to describe the website in presentations

> "We use a free static website as the project's public information portal and survey entry point. Data are collected through a restricted Google Forms/Sheets workflow. Vulnerability screening is performed and validated separately, and only approved aggregate map outputs are published. The website complements, but does not replace, supervised fieldwork, professional referrals, barangay validation and action planning."
