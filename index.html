ELECTRICAL SAFETY AWARENESS

Small habits can support safer homes.

General guidance for residents. These tips are not substitutes for a licensed electrician's assessment or emergency services.

01 / WARNING SIGNS

Recognize signs of danger

Take burning smells, sparks, smoke, damaged cords and unusually hot outlets seriously. Do not touch or test a suspected hazard.

02 / SAFE USAGE

Use outlets responsibly

Avoid daisy-chaining extensions or overloading sockets. Use electrical products as rated and replace visibly damaged accessories.

03 / PREPAREDNESS

Know your exit and contacts

Discuss evacuation routes with your family and ensure everyone knows how to request emergency assistance.

04 / PROFESSIONAL HELP

Refer technical concerns

Electrical panel work, diagnosis, repairs and energized testing should be handled by appropriately qualified personnel.

There is an immediate danger or active fire?

Move to safety, avoid contact with electrical equipment, and contact local emergency responders. This project website does not receive emergency reports.

PROJECT SCOPE

What this project is — and is not

Voluntary needs assessment and nonintrusive observation
Privacy-conscious aggregate screening and planning
Stakeholder-led validation, training and turnover
Not an electrical inspection or code-compliance certificate
Not a diagnosis of fire causation or individual home safety
Not an emergency response or hazard-reporting system
